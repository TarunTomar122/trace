India’s AI boom pushes firms to trade near-term revenue for users

India's AI firms shift focus from free users to revenue

India's AI market is booming, with generative AI app downloads surging 207% year-over-year in 2025, making it the largest market globally.
Companies like OpenAI and Google are ending free promotions to convert users into paying subscribers, facing monetization challenges despite high download rates.
As promotional offers wane, the real test for these firms will be converting their massive user base into sustainable revenue.
