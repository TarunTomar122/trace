# Chrome Web Store listing

## Name

Trace: AI & Tech News in Every New Tab

## Summary

Open a new tab and catch up on fresh AI and technology news from Trace.

## Detailed description

Trace turns every new tab into a clean feed of current AI and technology news.

Trace reads 100+ tech sources and merges the coverage of each event into one story, so you see each story once with links to every report. Stories are ranked the same way as on yourtrace.online, with the story of the day first. Filter by topic, open a story for the full Trace summary, or jump straight to the original reporting. Trace is free.

Features:

- One card per story: the same event from five outlets shows once, with "More on this story" links
- AI, markets, startups, technology, design, and robotics filters
- Every summary free, no limits
- Optional Google sign-in for bookmarks that sync with the Trace website
- A Saved filter for returning to stories later
- One-click sharing and direct original-source links
- A 10 minute local cache, so a new tab doesn't make a request every time
- Cached stories during temporary network failures
- Responsive light and dark themes

Privacy:

Trace does not read browsing history, inspect open tabs, inject scripts into websites, or track activity on other pages. Local storage keeps the story cache, account connection, and saved stories. Trace website access is used only for the news feed, Google account connection, and bookmark sync. The grid includes up to three cards marked "Sponsored" and a card for StocksBrew, another product from Trace's maker; sponsor links go through yourtrace.online so the click is counted, with no personal data. If you sign in, your saved stories sync with your Trace account. Public article thumbnails load from their listed image hosts.

## Category

News & Weather

## Single purpose

Replace the browser's new-tab page with a focused AI and technology news feed from Trace.

## Permission justification

`storage` saves the story cache, connected account session, and bookmarks. Access to `yourtrace.online` loads the feed, connects Google sign-in, and syncs bookmarks.
