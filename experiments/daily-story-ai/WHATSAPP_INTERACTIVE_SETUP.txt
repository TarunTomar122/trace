Getting WhatsApp Interactive Messages (Lists & Buttons)
========================================================

The interactive UI (list picker, reply buttons) you want is ONLY available with:
✅ Approved WhatsApp Business API
❌ NOT available in Twilio sandbox

## Current Status:
- ✅ Code is ready (I've implemented it)
- ❌ Interactive features won't work in sandbox
- ✅ Will work automatically once you get approved

## How to Get Approved:

### Option 1: Direct WhatsApp Business API (Meta)
**Best for:** Long-term production use
**Cost:** Free for first 1,000 conversations/month

Steps:
1. Create Facebook Business Manager account
2. Apply for WhatsApp Business API access
3. Verify your business (need business documents)
4. Get a dedicated phone number
5. Set up webhook (same code, different URL)

Apply here: https://business.facebook.com/wa/manage/home/

**Timeline:** 1-2 weeks for approval

### Option 2: Twilio WhatsApp Business
**Best for:** Quick production launch
**Cost:** $0.005/message + Twilio fees

Steps:
1. In Twilio Console, go to "Request Access" for WhatsApp Business
2. Submit business information
3. Wait for approval (faster than Meta direct)
4. Once approved, update TWILIO_WHATSAPP_NUMBER in .env
5. Set WHATSAPP_USE_INTERACTIVE=true in .env

**Timeline:** 3-5 days for approval

### Option 3: Other Providers
- MessageBird
- 360Dialog
- Gupshup

All require business verification.

## What You Need for Approval:

1. **Business Documents:**
   - Business registration
   - Tax ID / EIN
   - Website with privacy policy
   - Terms of service

2. **WhatsApp Business Profile:**
   - Business name
   - Business description
   - Business category
   - Business address
   - Business website

3. **Use Case Description:**
   - How you'll use WhatsApp
   - What messages you'll send
   - How users will opt-in

## For Trace App:

"Personalized news and research delivery platform. Users subscribe to receive daily curated news summaries based on their professional interests and career goals. Users can interact with the bot to request deep-dive analysis on any news story. All messages are opt-in and users can opt-out anytime by sending 'stop'."

**Message Templates You'll Need:**
1. Daily news digest (outbound)
2. Deep dive analysis (responsive)
3. Welcome message (triggered)

## To Enable Interactive Features:

Once approved, just add to your .env:
```
WHATSAPP_USE_INTERACTIVE=true
```

The code will automatically:
- Show list picker for headlines
- Show reply buttons for context options
- Handle button/list responses
